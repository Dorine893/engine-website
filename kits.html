Overseas STEM Kit Distribution

ENGINE distributes hands-on engineering kits to partner organizations, enabling local, instructor-led STEM workshops across multiple engineering disciplines.

3Workshop Kits per Package
30–40Students per Kit

What's in a Kit

Each kit includes detailed manuals, workshop materials, build components, and guided instructional resources.

These kits allow students to participate in high-quality engineering experiences even in communities with limited STEM resources.

Kits are currently in use with our partner organizations in Kenya, Zambia, and Lesotho, and are built to be run by local instructors without ENGINE staff on site.
